# ☀️ SOLAR ENERGY MONITOR

## Our project
We are building a small solar-powered house.

Our model uses solar panels to produce electricity and LED lights to demonstrate how solar energy can help us save electricity.

## Objectives
- Use renewable energy.
- Generate electricity with solar panels.
- Illuminate a miniature house.
- Measure energy production.
- Learn mathematics using PEMDAS.

## Materials
- Cardboard house
- 5V mini solar panel
- LED light
- 220 ohm resistor
- Electrical wires
- Digital multimeter

## How does it work?
1. The solar panel receives sunlight.
2. The panel produces electricity.
3. Electricity powers the LED.
4. The multimeter measures voltage.
5. We record and analyze the results.

## Mathematics: PEMDAS

Example:

(1 × 3) × (70 ÷ 100) = 2.1 Wh

This estimates the energy produced by a 1W panel in 3 hours, assuming 70% of its rated output.
